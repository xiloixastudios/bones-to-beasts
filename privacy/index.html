Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. This policy covers the Bones to Beasts website and the "Bones to Beasts posting" TikTok app, both run by Xiloixa Studios.

What the TikTok app does

The app is a tool used by the studio itself. It uploads gameplay videos as drafts to the studio's own TikTok account and reads that account's public video statistics (views, likes, comments and shares) to decide what to post next. It is not offered to the public and only accounts the studio authorises can sign in to it.

Data it accesses

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in TikTok account (open id, display name, avatar), through TikTok's Login Kit.
  • The list of that account's public videos and their counts, through TikTok's Display API.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account's inbox as drafts, through TikTok's Content Posting API.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and video statistics are stored only on a computer owned by the studio, used only to run the posting tool, and are never sold, shared or transferred to any third party. No data about other TikTok users is collected. No advertising or tracking is used on this site.

Deleting the data

Revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions) ends its access immediately. To have stored data deleted, email the address below.

The game

Bones to Beasts runs on Roblox. Player data inside the game is handled by Roblox under the Roblox Privacy Policy; the game stores only the progress needed to play (fossils, beasts, Gold and settings), tied to the Roblox account.
