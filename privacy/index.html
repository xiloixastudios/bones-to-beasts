Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026. This policy covers the Bones to Beasts website and the TikTok app named "Bones to Beasts posting". Both are run by Xiloixa Studios.

What the TikTok app does

The app is an internal tool. The studio uses it to upload gameplay videos to its own TikTok account as drafts, and to read that account's public video statistics (views, likes, comments and shares) so it can decide what to post next. The app is not offered to the public. Only accounts the studio has authorised can sign in.

What it can access

  • The signed-in account's basic profile: its open id, display name and avatar. This comes through TikTok's Login Kit.
  • The account's list of public videos and their counts. This comes through TikTok's Display API.
  • Permission to upload videos to the account's inbox as drafts. This comes through TikTok's Content Posting API.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and video statistics are stored on one computer owned by the studio. They are used only to run the posting tool. They are never sold, shared or passed to anyone else. The app collects nothing about other TikTok users. This website uses no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Deleting the data

You can end the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. To have stored data deleted, email the address below.

The game itself

Bones to Beasts runs on Roblox, so player data inside the game is handled by Roblox under the Roblox Privacy Policy. The game saves only what is needed to play: fossils, beasts, Gold and settings, tied to the player's Roblox account.
